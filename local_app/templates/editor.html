1. Draft alignment (runs on Modal GPU)

  

  

    
    
    
    
  

  

    Live recording? Put the timestamp where the singing really starts in
    Song starts at (e.g. 0:45). The intro is skipped before any
    timing runs, so the karaoke can't latch onto the talking — and the transcript
    won't contain it either. Leave it blank when the song starts at 0:00.
  

  
  

    Even with official lyrics, the AI transcribes the vocals first to find where each word
    is sung in the audio. It then anchors your lyrics to those timestamps. This is normal and
    necessary — without it, the system has no way to know when each word occurs.
  

  

    Uploading a lyric video (MP4 with lyrics burned in) works too: the vocals
    are separated from the instrumental, and the instrumental is put back onto your
    original video — the on-screen lyrics stay, the singer's voice is gone.
    The result is ready for download as soon as separation finishes (no editing).

    Drafts are slow by nature: your audio is uploaded, a Modal GPU server is booted,
    then the vocals are separated from the beat, transcribed, and aligned. The green bar
    shows the stage. First run after idle can take a few minutes — this is normal.
  







  
Check the transcript (fix the text, then align — word timing comes after)

  

    Transcription is rough — this is your chance to fix it before GPU time is spent on
    timing. Listen to your original audio above, fix the transcript in the lyrics
    box, then hit Run alignment.
  

  

    Live take? If the transcript starts with stage talk ("check check", "what's up guys")
    rather than the song, delete those lines and put the timestamp where the song
    really starts in Song starts at (step 1, e.g. 0:45) — otherwise the
    aligner still hears that intro and can pin your first lines onto it.
